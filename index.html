About SkidRush

SkidRush is an independent mobile game development studio dedicated to building high-quality, engaging narrative experiences for Android players worldwide. Our mission is to blend rich visual storytelling with interactive choices that put players right in the center of compelling journeys.

Privacy Policy

At SkidRush, we respect your privacy. Our mobile games may use standard third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) to display ads. These networks may collect anonymous usage data and device identifiers to provide relevant advertising experiences in accordance with Google Play policies.
