Privacy policy

This summary explains general privacy practices for SkidRush’s website and games. Individual app listings may have additional details.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

About this policy

SkidRush (“we,” “our,” or “us”) develops and publishes mobile games. This policy describes, at a general level, how information may be handled when you visit this website or use our games. The information and features used can differ between apps and may change as an app is updated.

Information and third-party services

Our games may use third-party services for functions such as advertising, analytics, app performance, or in-app purchases. Depending on the app and the services enabled, these providers may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance data. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Advertising and analytics

Some games may display ads or use third-party SDKs. Such services may collect or receive data to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, or understand app usage. Please review the Data safety section of the relevant Google Play listing for the current app-specific disclosures.

How information is used

Where applicable, information may be used to operate and maintain the games, support core features, troubleshoot issues, improve stability, measure engagement, provide advertising, and meet legal or platform requirements.

Children’s privacy

Our games are published with age ratings shown on their respective store listings. Parents and guardians should review those listings and the relevant third-party service policies before allowing children to use an app. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a concern, contact us so we can review it.

Data retention, security, and your choices

Data handling and retention depend on the app and the service provider. Third-party services may offer privacy controls through device settings or their own tools. We encourage you to review your device’s advertising and privacy settings, as well as the disclosures in Google Play. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

External links and changes

This website links to third-party services such as Google Play and GitHub. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use them. We may update this policy when our apps, services, or legal requirements change. The date above indicates when this page was last revised.
